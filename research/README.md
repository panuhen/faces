# Research

Background research behind Face Feelings, collected while designing the game (November 2026).

- [Face tracking](face-tracking.md): libraries and models for reading facial expressions from a camera, why the game uses MediaPipe blendshapes, what works and what doesn't, and the EU AI Act framing.
- [Prior games and studies](prior-games.md): earlier games and trainings for children with autism or other special needs, what they found, and what we took from them.

These are working notes, not a systematic review. Where we could not read a source in full, the notes say so. Nothing here is legal or medical advice.
